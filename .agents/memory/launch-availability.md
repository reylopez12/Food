Partner applications and listing claims are paused while this part is being set
up to better connect restaurants to the community. Keep the public partner route
as a setup notice rather than an application form; preserve existing inquiries
and their admin access.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested disabling the partner page during setup.

**How to apply:** Do not reopen the page or accept new public partner submissions
without the user's direction. Reopening requires coordinated website and API
changes, not just restoring the visible form.

Restaurant/city counts should include “More coming soon.” Food trucks should
remain discoverable with a “Coming soon” notice explaining that favorite trucks
will be findable in one place.

**Why:** The user requested this launch messaging without providing a launch date.

**How to apply:** Retain accurate existing counts and listings; do not invent truck
locations, new inventory or a launch date to support the announcement.
